The Sticker Weekly
Week of May 25, 2026
The DTC brand that once stood for radical transparency just sold to the fast-fashion giant it was built to oppose — Everlane's $100M exit to Shein closes the book on a founding myth of the 2010s startup era. While that chapter ends, the next is accelerating: AI-native consumer platforms, metabolic health infrastructure, and creator-led beverage brands are all capturing serious capital this week. The common thread is distribution advantage — whether through a chatbot in Snap's inbox, a dietitian network covering 200M insured lives, or a TikTok influencer's 10 million followers.
🔀 M&A
Mergers & Acquisitions
👗 Everlane Sold to Shein for $100M #news-searcher
  • L Catterton sold Everlane — the 2011-founded "radical transparency" DTC pioneer — to Shein for a reported $100M, barely enough to cover its $90M in outstanding debt. Common shareholders received no payout.
  • The exit follows Allbirds' $39M fire sale to American Exchange Group earlier this year, completing a grim pattern: the cohort of sustainability-forward, values-driven DTC brands from the 2010s consistently failed to translate moral positioning into pricing power or margin.
  • Shein — the ultra-fast fashion giant Everlane was philosophically founded to oppose — will operate it as an independent subsidiary. The acquisition is in part a playbook move: Shein gains a premium halo brand and U.S. brand equity precisely as it faces regulatory and reputational pressure.
  • The broader signal: value-forward challengers like Quince (raised $500M at a $10.1B valuation) are now winning by competing ruthlessly on price, not purpose — a definitive verdict on which consumer positioning survives long-term.
Trend: Millennial DTC brands that over-indexed on values-based identity — without ruthless cost discipline — are being fire-sold as price-first competitors prove consumers ultimately prioritize value over principle.
Modern Retail
🌿 Unilever Bets $1.2B on Greens Gummies
  • Unilever agreed to acquire Grüns — a greens supplement gummy brand founded in 2023 — for $1.2B, marking a major strategic pivot following its spinoff of its food division and its ambition to become the dominant player in functional wellness DTC.
  • Grüns has amassed 1M+ customers and ships approximately 10 million gummies daily; it ranks as the #1 greens supplement on Amazon and holds wide retail distribution across Target, Walmart, and Costco — an exceptional omnichannel footprint for a 3-year-old brand.
  • The deal caps a deliberate Unilever portfolio reorientation: with ice cream being divested and food spun off, Beauty & Wellbeing is now its highest-margin growth engine, and Grüns-style functional DTC brands represent the future of the corporate identity.
  • Watch: this deal establishes a clear acquisition benchmark — brands that combine Amazon dominance, mass retail placement, and a defensible hero SKU with a "daily ritual" behavior are the most acquirable in today's CPG M&A environment.
Trend: Post-food-division CPG conglomerates are paying $1B+ premiums for DTC wellness brands that cracked the Amazon + mass retail dual-distribution code — telegraphing exactly which DTC exit profiles command top multiples.
Inc. Magazine
🥂 Sazerac Bets on Alix Earle's Sipmargs #news-searcher
  • Spirits giant Sazerac — maker of Fireball and BuzzBallz — acquired an equity stake and exclusive national distribution rights to Sipmargs, the canned margarita brand co-owned by TikTok mega-influencer Alix Earle and Palm Tree Crew.
  • This deal follows Sazerac's minority stake in Kendall Jenner's 818 Tequila just two weeks prior, revealing a deliberate dual-track strategy: acquire distribution leverage through creator-founded brands while gaining authentic Gen Z cultural access that legacy brands can't internally manufacture.
  • Sipmargs was founded in 2021, relaunched after a $3M round last year, and will now scale nationally through Sazerac's distribution network — essentially a national retail rocket for a brand that built its initial traction through social-first marketing and limited drops.
Trend: Legacy spirits distributors are acquiring minority stakes in creator-founded DTC beverage brands as the most capital-efficient path to Gen Z shelf space — turning influencer identity into national distribution leverage.

🤖 CONSUMER AI
Consumer AI Spotlight
🎮 Status AI Raises $17M to Kill the Passive Feed #things-to-read
  • Status AI — a gamified social app where users roleplay as characters inside AI-generated fictional worlds — raised $17M in combined seed and Series A from General Catalyst, Y Combinator, LightShed Partners, and Abstract.
  • The platform has seen 13 million worlds and 5 million character profiles created since launch, with an early user base skewing young women — historically the demographic that identifies breakout social platforms before mainstream adoption catches up.
  • Founders argue that passive scrolling is the dying paradigm and the next category is immersive, AI-powered social entertainment — the raise is drawing attention from studios and streaming platforms seeing potential for IP franchise creation from user-generated social worlds.
Trend: The next social platform cycle may belong to AI-native apps that turn users from passive content consumers into active characters — replacing the algorithm-curated feed with participatory world-building mechanics.
TechCrunch
📱 Snapchat Puts Brand AI Agents in Your Chat
  • Snapchat launched AI Sponsored Snaps — placing brand AI agents directly in Snap's Chat tab — giving any brand access to a surface where 85% of Snap's ~1 billion monthly active users engage regularly, with 950 billion chats sent in Q1 2026 alone.
  • Brands deploy their own AI agents to answer product questions, make recommendations, and drive purchases without users leaving the conversation; the format already shows 22% more conversions and ~20% lower cost-per-action versus standard Sponsored Snaps in early tests.
  • The alpha launched with Experian, but the format is purpose-built for consumer brands — effectively turning Snap's chat into a persistent, conversational DTC storefront that lives inside the most-used tab on the app.
Trend: Consumer social platforms are converting high-frequency chat interfaces into AI-native commerce surfaces — fundamentally changing how DTC brands acquire, engage, and convert customers at scale without paid search dependence.
TechCrunch
🏋️ Hims & Hers Builds an AI Agent for Weight Loss
  • Hims & Hers announced an upcoming AI companion agent for weight-loss support — its second AI agent after the biomarker-interpreting Labs AI — as part of a broader platform re-architecture around modular AI agents covering the full patient journey.
  • The pivot comes as the business navigates a post-GLP-1-shortage compression: Q1 2026 revenue grew just 4% YoY to $608M — down sharply from 69% growth in 2024 — after the FDA restored Wegovy/Ozempic availability and eliminated compounding pharmacy arbitrage.
  • Hims is rebuilding its moat through AI personalization: 40 dedicated AI team members, a new CTO, and a Novo Nordisk partnership now delivering branded Wegovy/Ozempic with 125K+ shipments in its first 6 weeks — coupling clinical access with AI-powered adherence support.
Trend: Telehealth DTC brands that scaled on GLP-1 compounding arbitrage must rebuild durable moats around AI-powered personalization and adherence before branded pharma commoditizes the prescribing layer.

Funding Rounds
🧬 Nourish Raises $100M Series C for AI Metabolic Care #things-to-read
  • Nourish raised $100M in Series C funding led by Menlo Ventures at a $1.75B valuation — bringing total raised to $215M — for its AI-native metabolic health platform that combines virtual registered dietitian care with GLP-1 prescribing and medication management.
  • The platform now works with 10,000+ registered dietitians, has completed millions of patient appointments (tripling YoY), and holds payer partnerships covering more than 200 million insured lives — an unusually broad insurance footprint for a 4-year-old startup that gives it built-in distribution rivals can't easily replicate.
  • Nourish's thesis: GLP-1 medication outcomes improve dramatically with sustained nutrition coaching alongside the drug — it is positioning itself as the behavioral infrastructure layer that maximizes drug efficacy and reduces adherence churn across the entire metabolic health stack.
Trend: The GLP-1 wave is creating a high-value second-order opportunity in AI-powered nutrition and metabolic coaching platforms that improve drug outcomes — and the most defensible moat is payer network coverage, not consumer brand.
Axios
🍹 Suja Life Goes Public, Raises $187M in IPO
  • Suja Life — maker of cold-pressed Suja juice, Vive Organic wellness shots, and Slice functional soda — priced its IPO at $21/share on May 7, raising $186.7M, but shares dropped 14% on day one and have since slid to ~$16 by mid-May.
  • The company holds 47% market share in U.S. cold-pressed juice and 42% in wellness shots, with $327M in 2025 revenue (+26% YoY) and a vertically integrated 270,000 sq ft California production facility — but still carries $23.3M in net losses.
  • Proceeds fund production capacity expansion and potential M&A; the weak first-day performance signals that public markets reward wellness growth narratives only when paired with a credible profitability timeline — top-line scale and category leadership alone are no longer sufficient for a premium IPO multiple.
Trend: The public market window for wellness consumer brands has reopened but remains tight on valuation — investors are price-disciplined and will punish growth-at-all-costs narratives without a near-term path to profitability.
Food Dive
☕ Proper Wild Raises $10M to Bet on Energy Gummies #things-to-read
  • Clean energy brand Proper Wild raised $10M to accelerate the launch of its caffeinated gummies — 100mg organic caffeine + 120mg L-theanine per serving — scaling the format alongside its existing energy shots business.
  • Proper Wild positions itself as the health-conscious alternative in the $22B+ energy drink market: plant-based, no artificial ingredients, 2x the caffeine of an espresso shot and 15x the L-theanine of a cup of green tea, in a portable gummy format purpose-built for on-the-go dosing.
  • The raise reflects a broader category format shift: consumer energy is fragmenting away from large-format RTD cans toward shots, gummies, and stick packs — a trend that mirrors health-forward CPG broadly, where portability and precise functional stacking are creating new white space for DTC-first brands.
Trend: The energy segment is undergoing a format revolution as health-conscious consumers shift from sugary RTDs to functional, dose-controlled gummies — opening a new DTC wedge for clean-ingredient brands that can own the format before Big Energy copies it.
